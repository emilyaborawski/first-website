About Me

Nearly 15 years of experience in applied research across public and private sectors, including internal and external consulting. I'm a principal research scientist at HumRRO, a non-profit dedicated to helping organizations make better decisions about people and work. My work spans the full research cycle, from collecting and managging data, to analyzing, visualizing, and communicating results in ways that drive meaningful change. I specialize in data storytelling, project management, and building inclusive teams through authentic leadership

I specialize in R, RMarkdown, and Quarto for reproducible workflows, and use Power BI to build interactive dashboards. I enjoy mentoring colleagues and building collaborative, inclusive teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of research do you focus on?

My work sits at the intersection of education, workforce development, and government program evaluation. I lead and contribute to implementation studies, alignment studies, and psychometric analyses.

What tools do you use for data analysis?

Primarily R, along with RMarkdown and Quarto for reproducible reporting. For interactive dashboards, I work in Power BI.

Are you open to collaboration or mentoring?

Yes. Mentoring early-career researchers and building inclusive, collaborative teams is a genuine priority, not just a line on a resume.
